# Project Context — Cell # One

## Overview
Build and maintain a professional commercial website for Cell # One, an authorized Movistar agent focused on:
- PYMES.
- Natural persons.

The website must help convert visitors into WhatsApp leads and contact form submissions.

## Brand Identity
- Company name: Cell # One.
- Logo file: assets/logocellone.png.
- Visual style: modern, corporate, telecom, trustworthy.
- Use the logo in the header, footer, and favicon if possible.
- Keep the logo undistorted and aligned with the brand palette.

## Business Model
Cell # One offers:
- Movistar activations.
- Monthly commercial promotions.
- Plans and services for PYMES.
- Plans and services for natural persons.
- Phones and accessories from recognized brands.

## Core Audience
Primary users:
- Business owners and managers looking for telecom solutions.
- Individuals looking for plans, activations, or devices.
- Users who need to review requirements before contacting sales.

## Site Goals
The site must:
- Generate leads through WhatsApp and forms.
- Clearly separate PYMES and natural persons.
- Present monthly promotions in an easy-to-update way.
- Explain requirements and documents for activations.
- Display phones and accessories clearly.
- Build trust and make contact easy.

## Required Sections
The website should include:
1. Header with logo, navigation, and WhatsApp CTA.
2. Hero section with value proposition and main action buttons.
3. Quick access for PYMES and natural persons.
4. Monthly promotions section.
5. Services section.
6. Requirements and documents section.
7. Phones and accessories catalog.
8. FAQ section.
9. Contact form section.
10. Footer with address, hours, social links, and legal info.

## Content Rules
- Do not invent promotions, prices, requirements, or conditions.
- Use placeholders when business data is missing.
- Keep copy short, commercial, and clear.
- Differentiate PYMES and natural persons throughout the site.
- Explain required documents in simple language.

## Design Rules
- Use the logo colors as the base palette.
- Keep the layout clean and uncluttered.
- Prioritize mobile usability.
- Keep CTAs visible and easy to click.
- Repeat the logo in the header and footer.
- Preserve logo proportions at all times.

## Technical Preferences
- Use HTML, CSS, and JavaScript unless otherwise specified.
- Prefer maintainable structure and accessible markup.
- Use reusable components when possible.
- Keep promotional content easy to edit monthly.

## Editing Guidance
If promotional data changes, update only the relevant section content.
If brand assets change, update files in /assets and adjust references accordingly.
If the structure grows, split page sections into modular files.

## Working Style
Before making major changes:
- Review the current structure.
- Preserve existing branding unless instructed otherwise.
- Prefer minimal, safe edits over unnecessary rewrites.
- Ask for clarification if required business information is missing.
